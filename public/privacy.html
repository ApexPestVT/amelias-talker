My Turn: how we handle your information

Last updated 19 November 2026. This page describes what the app does today. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Who this is for

My Turn is set up by a parent, guardian or other adult. Children do not create accounts and are never asked for information.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your email address, so we can send you a sign-in code and you can get your setup back on another tablet.
  • For each child you add: an age range (for example "6 to 8"), the kind of condition you choose from a list, and a first name or nickname if you type one. Every one of these can be answered "Prefer not to say" or left empty.
  • Each device you sign in on: a name for it (such as "iPad"), what kind of device it is, and its setup: the settings, the words you added, and the answers you typed in "About your child" (such as the names of family members, pets, foods and places).
  • Photos you added in the app (pictures for words and photo scenes), so they can be copied to another of your devices. You can switch this off under "Account and saved setup"; photos already saved for that device are then removed from our database.
  • When you signed in and when the setup was last saved.

What we do not store

  • Photos from anywhere else on the device. Only photos you put into My Turn are saved, and only while "Save this device's photos too" is switched on.
  • Recordings of anyone's voice. See "Listening" below.
  • Passwords. There are none.
  • Exact birth dates or medical records.

Why we store it

  • To save each device's setup and copy it to another of your devices when you ask.
  • To tell the people and organizations who fund My Turn how many children it helps, in which age ranges, with which kinds of conditions, and on how many devices. These are totals only. No names, emails, saved words or photos are ever part of a report.

We do not sell this information, show advertising, or use it for marketing.

Listening, voices and pictures

If listening is switched on, what the microphone hears is sent to a speech service to be turned into words, so the app can offer answers. If "AI help" is switched on, the question that was heard and the words on the board are sent to an AI service to choose answer options. If a child voice is chosen, the words to be spoken are sent to a voice service. Symbol pictures come from the ARASAAC library. My Turn does not keep recordings, and these services are sent no name or email.

Where it is kept

On a database run for My Turn by Railway, in the United States. Sign-in codes are emailed through Resend.

Your choices

  • See or change it: lock menu, 1. Stage and name, "Account and saved setup".
  • Delete it: in the same place, "Delete my account and everything saved". This removes your email, every child you added, every device, and every saved setup and photo from our database straight away.
  • Sign out: the tablet keeps working with what is on it.
