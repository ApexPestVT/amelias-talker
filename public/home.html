A free voice for kids who can't speak yet

My Turn To Talk is a free talking picture board. Tap a picture and the device says the word.

Download on the App Store Open in a browser

For iPad. Free, with no ads.

How it works

Tap to talk

Big pictures, one tap each. The device says the word out loud.

It hears the question

Ask a question out loud and the answers pop up, ready to tap.

It grows with your child

Six stages, from first words to full sentences.

Coming next

My Turn To-Go

A small wristband with four talking buttons, for the playground and everywhere a tablet can't go. It is being built and tested now and is not available yet.

Who we are

Built by a dad for his daughter

My Turn started with one family in Vermont. It is a fiscally sponsored project of the Givinga Foundation, and it is free for every family.

Help keep it free

Gifts keep the app free for every family and help get the wristband safely onto a child's wrist.
