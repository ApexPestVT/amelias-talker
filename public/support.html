My Turn To Talk: help and support

A free talking picture board for people who do not speak, or who need help being understood.

Contact us

Email support@myturntotalk.org. Tell us what kind of device you use (iPad, Android tablet, or a web browser) and what happened.

Getting the app

On an iPad, download My Turn To Talk from the App Store. On an Android tablet or a computer, open it in a web browser.

Opening the grown-ups menu

Press and hold the lock button in the top right corner for about 2 seconds. Everything a grown-up can change is in that menu.

Signing in

  • Type your email and tap "Email me a code". We send 6 numbers. There is no password.
  • The code works for 15 minutes. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.
  • Asking for a new code cancels the one before it, so use the newest email.
  • Signing in is optional on a device that is already in use. The board keeps working without it.

Using more than one device

Sign in with the same email on each device. Each device keeps its own setup. To copy a setup, added words and photos from one device to another, open the grown-ups menu, then 1. Stage and name, then "Account and saved setup".

It is not hearing questions

  • Check that listening is switched on in the grown-ups menu, under 2. Listening and questions.
  • Allow the microphone when the device asks.
  • Check the internet connection.

Deleting your account

Open the grown-ups menu, then 1. Stage and name, then "Account and saved setup", then "Delete my account and everything saved". This removes your email, every child you added, every device, and every saved setup and photo from our database straight away.

A note on speech therapy

My Turn To Talk does not replace a speech-language pathologist. Ask your therapist how it fits with their plan.
