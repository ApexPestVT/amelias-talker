How to teach with this

    

      
    
        
  1. Talk and tap. Say your sentence out loud and tap one or two key words on her board as you say them. "Time to EAT."
    2. 
        
  2. Use Show her. Hold the Show her button at the top for one second first. Your taps then speak the word and show it, but do not build a sentence and are not counted as her words. It turns itself off after 90 quiet seconds.
    3. 
        
  3. Stay one step ahead. If she uses one word, show two: WANT + EAT.
    4. 
        
  4. Ask, then wait. Count to five in your head before you help. Look at her, then at the board.
    5. 
        
  5. Answer every tap. Even an accident gets a real response. She taps UP, you say "You want up!" and lift her.
    6. 
        
  6. Add a little. She says EAT. You say and tap EAT + MORE.
    7. 
        
  7. Do not quiz. Skip "show me eat." Use it in real moments: meals, play, bath, the car.
    8. 
        
  8. Keep it in reach, always. Signs, pointing and sounds still count too.
    9.
